# V5 Visual QA

Date: 2026-06-10

## Passed

- Rendered 35 slides across 5 carousel drafts.
- Every exported slide is 1080x1350.
- Generator now rejects diagram/chart/mechanism files as background images.
- Generator now rejects product packshots as background images.
- Generator now rejects unsafe background aspect ratios instead of creating inset-photo pseudo-cards.
- Renderer allows only one overlay type: a small centered lower-space diagram from the Sonagi Reference diagram set.
- Renderer does not paste product packshots or second lifestyle photos on top of the slide.
- Main layout is more consistent: one centered full-bleed background photo, upper-left editorial text block, lower centered diagram when useful, clearer CTA strip.

## Still Needs Human/Iris Judgment

- The archive is not deep enough for every topic. Some slides use adjacent Sonagi-owned images because exact article images are limited.
- Future automation should request Iris/image lab when a carousel needs more than two adjacent/borrowed visuals.
- Best next Iris asks:
  - Double cleansing: owned balm/emulsification images, not generic face-washing.
  - Heartleaf/sebum: oily-but-healthy skin and calm redness imagery without repeating the same leaf texture.
  - Sephora Kids/SPF: tasteful SPF and simple-routine visuals that do not rely too heavily on child-face closeups.

## Non-Negotiable Learned From This Pass

Never use diagrams as background photos. Never paste product packshots or second lifestyle photos on top of Auteur-style photo slides. Diagrams are allowed only as small centered lower-space educational inserts. If the slide cannot be full-bleed and harmonious with one owned contextual image, mark it for Iris instead of forcing the asset.
