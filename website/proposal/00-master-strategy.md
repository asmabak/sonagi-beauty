## TL;DR — the 10 decisions that hold

1. **Sephora × Olive Young is NOT existential for France.** Phase 1 (autumn 2026) = US/Canada/HK/SEA. Phase 2 (2027) = UK/AU/ME. FR unannounced — verified via [Sephora Newsroom](https://newsroom.sephora.com/sephora-and-olive-young-partner-to-bring-the-best-of-korean-beauty-to-sephora-consumers/) and [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10660053). **Sonagi has minimum 18-month uncontested specialist-aisle window in France.**

2. **Sonagi is a CURATOR, not a hero-SKU DTC.** Revenue = retail margin on 104 partner-brand SKUs at launch (Anua, BoJ, COSRX, SKIN1004, Mixsoon, Numbuzin, Round Lab, Laneige, Medicube, TIRTIR, Mary&May, Biodance + halo). The moat is CURATION LOGIC + clinical context + founder voice + bilingual depth. Closer to Soko Glam / Caroline Hirons than Rhode.

3. **Asma is the photogenic curator-as-personality.** Not a credentialed glossaire-author expert (drop the SCS Diploma lead). Not Hailey-with-a-hero-SKU. She's the trusted French K-beauty curator. Marouane + Sarra support publicly.

4. **The FR Wikipedia entity layer is greenfield — the single most valuable editorial move.** [K-beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-beauty) has Wikipedia articles in 10 languages — **French is not one of them**. [PDRN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polydeoxyribonucleotide) is EN-only. Glass skin, mool-gwang, chok-chok, hanbang-as-cosmetic — all 404 in every language.

5. **AI citations don't overlap with Google rankings.** Only 12 % overlap between AI citations and Google's top 10 SERP ([iPullRank / Ahrefs 15,000-query study](https://ipullrank.com/probability-ai-search)). **4 of 5 AI citations come from pages with no SERP ranking at all.** Engineer for *citation*, not *ranking*.

6. **Zero of six French K-beauty competitors emit meaningful schema** (Article, Person, DefinedTerm, MedicalSubstance, HowTo, FAQPage). **Day-1 schema stack = uncontested AEO moat in 90 days.**

7. **The clinical-procedure crossover is uncontested in FR.** Query "rejuran post procedure skincare routine" returns **zero** FR specialist K-beauty citations. "PDRN saumon cosmétique" returns one MyLittleYuzu article. **This is the Sonagi USP made AEO-explicit.** Lead with this.

8. **One cultural-moment collab per year — never paid-influencer arrays.** Rhode did exactly this: Krispy Kreme (Aug 2023), Erewhon (Feb 2024). **Year 1: Sonagi × iconic French boulangerie or Le Bon Marché café.** Year 2: Sonagi × Galeries Lafayette pop-up.

9. **Curation-as-IP is the moat for Year 1.** Without an own SKU, the moat is HOW you select + WHO you cite + WHAT clinical context you add. The 104 partner SKUs are commodity inputs; the curation logic is the product.

10. **The 06:47 daily TikTok + Pacifico-yellow-Korean-script visual system + hand-written details (notes in shipments, silk-ribbon-tied orders, signed postcards from Asma) stay** — these are still the costly signals. The brand has photographable signatures even without a hero own-product.

## The board's verdict — revised after the founder pivot

### Seth Godin — *smallest viable market, permission, costly signaling*
> "The €87 kit was the wrong costly signal for a SVM that doesn't yet know you exist. The right costly signals for Year 1 are inside the experience: silk ribbon on every partner-brand shipment, hand-written postcard from Asma in the first 200 orders, banana-yellow tissue paper, Korean-script-on-French-cardboard. The CURATION DECISIONS are the costly signal — what Sonagi refuses to stock (no Glow Recipe US-made K-inspired) is as important as what it stocks."

### Ann Handley — *pathological empathy*
> "Without the hero kit, every glossaire page works harder. The voice doesn't change — open inside Camille's bathroom at 11pm. But now the conversion goes: 'Here's why centella matters → here are the 4 centella products I trust → use the diagnostic to find your fit.' The quiz is the bridge to commerce. Make it feel like a conversation, not a form."

### Neil Patel — *AEO/GEO is the only game now*
> "The AEO moat is intact and possibly stronger. Without a hero SKU to defend, Sonagi can put 100 % of editorial energy into the schema stack + entity layer + clinical-crossover content. Six of six FR competitors have left this open. Sonagi can be the top-cited FR source for K-beauty by day 90."

### Tim Ferriss — *muse-test, asymmetric leverage*
> "The muse-test simplifies. Without €87 to validate, run a €350 Meta campaign to the diagnostic quiz. Measure: quiz completion → email capture → first-product checkout. Simpler funnel, more data, faster iteration."

### Deep Patel — *mere exposure, behavioral compounding*
> "Mere exposure now compounds through the BRAND VISUAL SYSTEM — banana yellow + Pacifico + Korean script + silk ribbon. Every shipment, every glossaire page, every social post is a brand impression. Without a hero box to repeat, repeat the SYSTEM."

### Gary Vee — *day-trading attention, cadence > polish*
> "Pinterest. 50 pins/week minimum. Every quiz output, every clinical-crossover page, every Asma routine moment becomes a pin. Le Glossaire IS the Pinterest content engine. Cadence wins over polish."

### Rory Sutherland — *costly signaling, psycho-logic*
> "The 06:47 timing, the silk ribbon, the hand-written postcard, the Pacifico-yellow-Korean visual system — every one of these is irrational by spreadsheet logic. The continuity-probability signal — 'they'll still exist in 5 years' — comes from consistency of CURATION over years. Sonagi refuses to chase trends, refuses to stock K-inspired non-Korean, refuses to discount — these refusals are the costly signal."

### Mark Ritson — *category creation discipline*
> "Sonagi is creating *La Douceur Quotidienne* as a category. The curator's taste IS the category proposition. Every selection decision should reinforce the category. No hero SKU needed — the brand IS the curation."

### Where the board agrees (unanimous)
1. **The diagnostic quiz is the conversion engine.** Persistent CTA every page.
2. **AEO/GEO > blue-link SEO.** Schema-stack + entity layer + passage engineering = the moat.
3. **Pinterest 50 pins/week minimum.**
4. **Asma's photogenic founder-curator voice drives EMV.**
5. **Drop the 25-page glossaire ambition.** 27 surgical pages mapped to actual SKU coverage.
6. **Rhode playbook applies MINUS the hero product** — 1 cultural-moment collab/year, founder-as-user content, no paid-influencer army, DTC-only Year 1, EMV-as-north-star.
