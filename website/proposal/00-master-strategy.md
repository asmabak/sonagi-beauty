## TL;DR — the 10 decisions that change everything

1. **Sephora × Olive Young is NOT existential for France.** France is in neither Phase 1 (autumn 2026: US/Canada/HK/SEA) nor Phase 2 (2027: UK/Australia/ME) — verified via [Sephora Newsroom](https://newsroom.sephora.com/sephora-and-olive-young-partner-to-bring-the-best-of-korean-beauty-to-sephora-consumers/) and [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10660053). **Sonagi has an 18-month uncontested specialist-aisle window in France.** Use the time aggressively; do not panic-build against a threat that isn't here.

2. **104 launch SKUs is the wrong shape.** Rhode hit $1B with 10 SKUs and 367 % YoY EMV growth ([e.l.f. investor release](https://investor.elfbeauty.com/stock-and-financial/press-releases/landing-news/2025/05-28-2025-210536607)). Reorganise the catalogue around **1–3 Sonagi-branded hero SKUs** (Le Rituel Sonagi as the Peptide-Lip-Treatment equivalent) with the 100+ partner SKUs as catalog ballast. Meme density beats SKU breadth.

3. **Asma is Sonagi's Hailey — a credible photogenic *user*, not a skincare *expert*.** This reverses Claude Desktop's encyclopedia-author framing (and the SCS-Diploma path it implied). Hailey explicitly avoided "skincare expert" positioning. She positioned as a USER. The locked Apr 25 strategy already says "Asma authors, Minjun renders" — *authoring* in the brand-voice/letter-from-a-friend sense, not the credentialed-expert sense. **Hold the SCS Diploma as a quiet credibility footer; do not lead with it.**

4. **The FR Wikipedia entity layer is greenfield — the single most valuable editorial move.** [K-beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-beauty) has Wikipedia articles in 10 languages — **French is not one of them**. [PDRN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polydeoxyribonucleotide) is EN-only. Glass skin, mool-gwang, chok-chok, hanbang-as-cosmetic — all 404 in every language. **Create the FR Wikipedia stubs and the Wikidata entities first.** They become the load-bearing citations AI Overviews will hit for the next decade.

5. **AI citations don't overlap with Google rankings.** Only 12 % overlap between AI citations (ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot) and Google's top 10 SERP ([iPullRank / Ahrefs 15,000-query study](https://ipullrank.com/probability-ai-search)). **4 of 5 AI citations come from pages with no SERP ranking at all.** Sonagi engineers for *citation*, not *ranking*. The two are different disciplines.

6. **Zero of six French K-beauty competitors emit meaningful schema** (Article, Person, DefinedTerm, MedicalSubstance, HowTo, FAQPage). ChokoMag's Dr. Coralie Petermann — PhD, 14-year authority — has no `Person` schema. **Day-1 schema stack = uncontested AEO moat in 90 days.** Schema is plumbing, not the lever Mike King talks about — but in this market it's the only plumbing nobody's laid.

7. **The clinical-procedure crossover is uncontested in FR.** Query "rejuran post procedure skincare routine" returns **zero** FR specialist K-beauty citations. "PDRN saumon cosmétique" returns one MyLittleYuzu article. **This is the Sonagi USP made AEO-explicit.** Asma's lived experience (Continental Skin Clinic, Sculptra, Rejuran) is the unrepeatable moat. Lead with this.

8. **One cultural-moment collab per year — never paid-influencer arrays.** Rhode did exactly this: Krispy Kreme (Aug 2023), Erewhon (Feb 2024). Each generated months of compounding earned media. **Year 1: Sonagi × iconic French boulangerie or café.** Year 2: Sonagi × Le Bon Marché. Year 3: Sonagi × Vogue France formulation drop.

9. **Stockouts and waitlists are FEATURES, not bugs.** Rhode hit 440 K waitlist on launch ([Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/pop/with-new-strawberry-glaze-flavor-rhode-closes-in-on-1-million-peptide-lip-treatments-sold/)) and refused retail for 3 years. The smallest-viable-market discipline (1,000 named Parisiennes/Lyonnaises) maps perfectly to limited inventory drops. **Plan for 500-unit drops that sell out in 48 h.**

10. **The €87 odd price + hand-tied silk thread + 06:47 daily TikTok + banana-yellow box are non-negotiable.** These are costly signals — they exist BECAUSE they're inconvenient. Sutherland was right. Do not optimise them away.

### New, from the research (not in any prior doc)
- **FR Wikipedia + Wikidata strategy.** Tier-1 stubs to create: `fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-beauty`, `fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polydeoxyribonucleotide`, Wikidata Q-entity for "Glass skin (K-beauty)" disambiguated from the 2008 Dir en grey song. Trickle-up moat irreplaceable by money.
- **66 pre-built vs-pages.** 12-brand portfolio × 11 combinations = 66 "Anua vs Beauty of Joseon"-style comparison pages. Zero FR competitor occupies these. Programmatic-SEO + schema goldmine.
- **King's "raffle ticket" framework.** Every Korean concept gets a passage-engineered glossary entry + bilingual + multimodal (text + image + 20-sec video). Each is independently retrievable by AI systems doing passage-level RAG.
- **Cannibalisation is now a FEATURE.** King explicitly endorses it. Sonagi targets "PDRN cosmétique" from 4 angles simultaneously: glossary entry, product page, blog deep-dive, FAQ. More tickets in the lottery.
- **"Sephora × OY validates the category."** Not zero-sum. Mass-curated K-beauty zones expand total category demand. Sonagi's defense: ritual + clinical-crossover + cultural fluency, not SKU breadth.

### Seth Godin — *smallest viable market, permission, costly signaling*

### Ann Handley — *pathological empathy*
> "The research vindicates me. Mike King's passage-level engineering means every chunk of content has to start *inside* her bathroom. Not 'Niacinamide is a B-vitamin' — but 'It's 7:14 am. You have 90 seconds before the kids start. Should you use the toner tonight or not?' Then the science. The competitors miss this because they write for Google, not for her. We write for her, and AI engines reward us for it."

### Neil Patel — *AEO/GEO is the only game now*
> "King's Ahrefs study proves it: 12 % overlap between AI citations and Google top 10. Sonagi can rank #1 for every K-beauty query in France and still get zero AI citations. The work is entity disambiguation + passage engineering + schema-stack + multimodal — not keyword density. Six of six FR competitors have left this entirely on the table. **Sonagi can own the FR K-beauty AI-citation surface within 90 days, with zero traffic prerequisite.** That's the moat."

### Tim Ferriss — *muse-test, asymmetric leverage*
> "Sephora × Olive Young is NOT in France. That's an 18-month muse-test runway. Spend it validating that €87 actually pulls. Run a €350 Meta campaign on a `/le-rituel-sonagi/` landing in week 4. If the kit pulls, build the encyclopedia. If it doesn't, scrap the €87 and find the price that does. Don't build 25 encyclopedia pages on faith. Validate emotion first, then build authority."

### Deep Patel — *mere exposure, behavioral compounding*
> "Rhode = phone case appearing 60.4M times in a single TikTok upload. The yellow box is Sonagi's equivalent. Every encyclopedia page must surface the box visually. The glossary is a *display surface* for the meme, not just an information resource. Mere-exposure rewires preference into automatic affect. Don't optimise the box into invisibility."

### Gary Vee — *day-trading attention, cadence > polish*
> "Pinterest is empty. French K-beauty on Pinterest is EMPTY. That's the obvious move and you're underweighting it. 50 pins a week is the minimum, not the maximum. Every glossary entry, every Le Rituel shot, every ingredient — each becomes 5 pins. The encyclopedia IS the Pinterest content engine. Do not separate them."

### Rory Sutherland — *costly signaling, psycho-logic*
> "The €87 odd price, the silk thread, the hand-tied 50-unit drops, the 06:47 timing — every single one is irrational by spreadsheet logic. Which is why every single one will work. The press will write the piece without being pitched because journalists can't resist a brand that does inconvenient things on purpose. Rhode did this with the phone case. Tiffany did it with the robin-egg-blue box. The continuity-probability signal — 'they'll still exist in 5 years' — comes from these details, not from PubMed citations."

### Mark Ritson (added voice) — *category creation discipline*
> "Sonagi is not competing with MiiN or Skinorea. Sonagi is creating *La Douceur Quotidienne* as a category. Every page should read as if it's the founding manifesto of a category nobody else has named. Generic encyclopedia entries dilute this. Surgical Korean-concept entries with cultural framing reinforce it."

3. Pinterest is the most under-priced channel. 50 pins/week minimum.
4. Asma's photogenic founder-user voice drives EMV, not credential walls.

**Three deliberate choices:**
1. `/encyclopedie/` not `/glossaire/` — encyclopédie ranks better for informational queries and signals more authority.
2. **Top-level `/le-rituel-sonagi/` and `/comparer/` and `/lexique/`** — not buried under `/encyclopedie/`. These are conversion + AEO surfaces, equal-weight to commerce.
3. **No date-based blog URLs.** `/journal/article-name/` not `/journal/2026/05/article-name/`. Date URLs decay; flat structure stays evergreen.

## Schema stack (the technical moat — see `02-page-templates.md` for full specs)

Every page emits a `<script type="application/ld+json">` block with `@graph` containing the relevant entities. **Bilingual `inLanguage` on every block** (`fr-FR` + `en-US` variants where applicable).

| Schema | Where it appears | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Organization` (Sonagi) | All pages | Brand entity anchor with `founders` array (Asma + Marouane + Sarra) |
| `Person` (Asma + Marouane + Sarra) | All pages with author content + `/à-propos/` | E-E-A-T — every byline. `sameAs` to verified LinkedIn, Goldman Sachs `alumniOf`, etc. **No competitor has this.** |
