## SECTION 1 — iPullRank / Mike King: Relevance Engineering as the foundational AEO framework

Mike King (Founder/CEO, [iPullRank](https://ipullrank.com)) coined and operationalized **Relevance Engineering** — the discipline replacing traditional SEO in the AI-search era. He was named [2025 Search Marketer of the Year](https://ipullrank.com/sel-search-marketer-of-the-year-2025) and runs [SEO Week 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/seo-week-2026-brings-ai-195300269.html) (April 27-30, NYC). His thesis is that AI search is **probabilistic, not deterministic**, which inverts every assumption built into legacy SEO tooling and KPIs.

### Definition

Relevance Engineering is the convergence of artificial intelligence, content strategy, information retrieval, UX, and digital PR ([Advanced Web Ranking](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/optimizing-new-search-how-relevance-engineering-is-reshaping-seo)). It is "supported by measurable systems that include vector embeddings, cosine similarity, topic segmentation, and semantic scoring at scale." Where SEO is checklist-based, Relevance Engineering is engineering-rigorous — King's blunt framing: "Do you all really want to stay the janitors of the web?" ([iPullRank SEO Week 2025](https://ipullrank.com/seo-week-2025-mike-king)).

### Five framework components

1. **Passage-level optimization** — AI systems retrieve passages, not pages. "There's no SEO tool out there that does that" ([AWR](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/optimizing-new-search-how-relevance-engineering-is-reshaping-seo)). Every paragraph must stand alone: claim + evidence + context.
2. **Query fan-out anticipation** — One user query expands into **9 distinct synthetic query types** in Google's system, "dozens in AI Mode versus a handful in AI Overviews" ([AWR](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/optimizing-new-search-how-relevance-engineering-is-reshaping-seo)). PROMPTAGATOR generates up to **8 synthetic queries per document at temperature 0.7** ([iPullRank Probability in AI Search](https://ipullrank.com/probability-ai-search)).
3. **Semantic coverage + entity clustering** — Cover terminology, synonyms, and related entities beyond head terms.
4. **Multimodal "raffle-ticket" strategy** — "You want to buy as many tickets as possible to increase your chances of being picked." Text + images + video + structured data = more retrieval hooks.
5. **New measurement** — citation rate, attribution count, retrieval confidence, mindshare/impression ratio. Position-based rank tracking "cannot capture this dynamic."

### Measurable claims (the load-bearing stats)

- **Only 12% overlap** between citations made by ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot vs Google's top 10 SERP (Ahrefs study of 15,000 long-tail queries cited in [iPullRank Probability paper](https://ipullrank.com/probability-ai-search)). Translation: ranking #1 in classic SEO does not get you into AI Overviews.
- **4 out of 5 AI citations** point to pages with no SERP ranking presence at all ([iPullRank Probability](https://ipullrank.com/probability-ai-search)).
- **Over 60% of AI citation attributions contain errors** (Tow Center study, cited [iPullRank Probability](https://ipullrank.com/probability-ai-search)).
- **~85% of AI Overview citations come directly from the SERP** today — but King flags this won't hold ([iPullRank SEO Week](https://ipullrank.com/seo-week-2025-mike-king)).
- **20-60% organic traffic loss** projected from AI Overviews — King says "exactly what we're seeing now" ([AWR](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/optimizing-new-search-how-relevance-engineering-is-reshaping-seo)).
- **20% click increase** from appending top-performing keywords to title tags (iPullRank internal testing, [SEO Week](https://ipullrank.com/seo-week-2025-mike-king)) — disproves "60-70 char title" dogma.
- **98% of traffic** carries value even without immediate conversion (brand memorability) ([AWR](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/optimizing-new-search-how-relevance-engineering-is-reshaping-seo)).

### What no longer works

- Position-based rank tracking ("cannot capture this dynamic")
- Single-keyword/head-term focus
- Domain authority alone
- Keyword cannibalization avoidance — King now **endorses** cannibalization because multiple passages targeting related intents increase citation probability
- Blog-post-only strategies (move to white papers, research, original data)
- Bolting GEO onto legacy SEO without organizational restructuring

### What works

- Passage-level content engineering (concise claim, evidence, context per chunk)
- Precise language: "SEO increases organic traffic" beats "helps get traffic"
- Original statistics + exclusive insights (citation magnets)
- Structured data enters the RAG pipeline at processing time (not training) — confirmed by King and Crystal Carter ([iPullRank SEO Week](https://ipullrank.com/seo-week-2025-mike-king))
- Contextual brand mentions (semantic context > co-occurrence)
- Agentic SEO systems (automated monitoring of relevance scores, auto-adjust content)
- Vector indexing of your own corpus (LlamaIndex etc.) to simulate retrieval

### Schema/entity disambiguation

King does NOT publish specific JSON-LD recipes in his flagship content. The signal: structured data is plumbing, not the lever. Authority comes from machine-readable authorship + citations + original data, not from schema completeness. (Implication: schema is necessary but insufficient — the real moat is semantic coverage + entity density.)

### Sonagi-specific implications

> **The Sonagi playbook from King's framework:**
> 1. **Build a passage-engineered glossary.** Every Korean concept (mool-gwang, sok-su-bun, glass skin, PDRN) gets a standalone passage with definition + provenance + bilingual translation. Each passage is independently retrievable. This converts Sonagi into a "citation magnet" for FR K-beauty queries.
> 2. **Pre-commit to query fan-out for 9 synthetic intents per product.** For Le Rituel Sonagi: definition, comparison, price, how-to, side effects, alternatives, ingredients, reviews, expert opinion. Each gets its own passage on the product/glossary pages.
> 3. **Deliberately cannibalize.** Multiple pages targeting "PDRN cosmetique" (product page, glossary entry, blog deep-dive, FAQ) = more raffle tickets in AI citation lottery.
> 4. **Multimodal day 1.** Every glossary term gets text + visual + ideally short video. Bilingual EN+FR doubles retrieval surface.
> 5. **Original data is the wedge.** Sonagi has the clinical-procedure crossover angle (Sculptra/Rejuran/Profhilo) — no FR competitor has this. Publish original protocols, before/after data, expert interviews. This is what gets cited when AI Overviews answer "rejuran post procedure skincare routine."
> 6. **Measurement: track AI citations weekly** in ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity for the 50 priority FR queries. Mindshare > rank position.

### Analysis (the moat thesis)

The French K-beauty competitor set has a **near-zero machine-readable identity layer**. Across 5 verifiable competitors:

- **0 of 5 emit Person/Author schema** with sameAs links to Wikidata, ORCID, or social profiles
- **0 of 5 emit FAQPage schema** despite all having product-comparison content
- **0 of 5 emit DefinedTerm or MedicalSubstance schema** for the Korean concepts (glass skin, PDRN, hanbang) they repeatedly use
- **0 of 5 emit HowTo schema** for routine articles
- **Only Shopify/PrestaShop defaults** (Product, Offer, sometimes AggregateRating) appear to be in play — and even those weren't surfaced by WebFetch's markdown conversion

The most striking case is **chokomag.com** ([Dr. Coralie Petermann](https://chokomag.com/a-propos/), France's #1 K-beauty editorial authority, PhD in Sciences, 14-year publication history, working with the Korean government since 2020). Her [Beauty of Joseon review](https://chokomag.com/skincare-coreenne-kbeauty/beauty-of-joseon-avis/) is exactly the kind of page AI engines would want to cite — except it has no Article schema, no Author Person schema, no sameAs to her credentials. From an AI crawler's perspective, she is invisible as an entity.

Per [Mike King's Relevance Engineering framework](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/optimizing-new-search-how-relevance-engineering-is-reshaping-seo), structured data enters the RAG pipeline at processing time. The competitor set is leaving AI-citation eligibility entirely on the table.

### Sonagi-specific implications

> **Where Sonagi must out-schema:**
>
> 1. **Person schema for the founder trio.** Day 1: Asma Bakhtar (sameAs Goldman Sachs LinkedIn, Wikidata if creatable), Marouane (sameAs TPay), Sarra (sameAs retail credentials). This is the single biggest gap — no competitor has this.
> 2. **DefinedTerm schema for every Korean concept.** Glass skin, mool-gwang, sok-su-bun, PDRN, hanbang, chok-chok, dohwasaek. Each glossary entry gets DefinedTerm + isPartOf DefinedTermSet "Korean Skincare Concepts." This becomes a **citation magnet** when AI Overviews answer "what is glass skin" in FR.
> 3. **MedicalSubstance schema for PDRN, Sculptra, Rejuran, Profhilo**. These are clinical compounds. Sonagi's clinical-procedure crossover angle gets schema-level legitimacy no competitor can match.
> 4. **FAQPage schema on every product + concept page.** 5-7 Q&A blocks per page targeting King's 9-intent query fan-out (definition, comparison, price, how-to, side effects, alternatives, ingredients, reviews, expert opinion).
> 5. **HowTo schema for Le Rituel Sonagi.** Step-by-step morning ritual with HowToStep + HowToSupply (each product) + HowToTool. Direct retrieval format for "comment commencer une routine k-beauty."
> 6. **Article + Author Person schema on every editorial piece** with sameAs to founder LinkedIn, Wikidata Q-entity (create), and credible co-author dermatologists/estheticians where used.
> 7. **AggregateRating + Review schema** with verifiable provenance (link to source platform — Trustpilot, Avis-Vérifiés, Yotpo).
> 8. **Organization schema** with founders array, founding date Sept 2026, foundingLocation Paris, knowsAbout array including all Korean concepts + clinical compounds.
> 9. **Speakable schema** for the glossary definitions — voice-assistant ready.
> 10. **Bilingual schema:** `inLanguage` on every block, both `fr-FR` and `en-US` versions. Most FR competitors don't tag language because they're FR-only.
>
> **Net positioning:** Within 90 days of launch Sonagi can be the only French K-beauty site with full DefinedTerm + MedicalSubstance + Person + HowTo + FAQPage stack. This is the most defensible AEO/GEO moat available — it is literally a code-level gap that requires no traffic, no brand, no inventory to close.

### Why this matters for AEO/GEO

Per [Mike King's framework](https://www.advancedwebranking.com/blog/optimizing-new-search-how-relevance-engineering-is-reshaping-seo), entity disambiguation runs through Wikidata. AI engines use Wikipedia/Wikidata as **the authoritative entity layer** behind knowledge panels and AI Overview citations. When Wikipedia is missing an article and Wikidata is missing an entity, the AI engine constructs the entity ad-hoc from whatever websites it cites — meaning **the next 12-24 months of citations will hardcode whatever authority emerges first into the knowledge graph**.
